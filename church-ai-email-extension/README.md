# ChurchAI Outreach Extension

An unofficial, high-productivity Chrome Manifest V3 extension designed for Adebusola Tomiwa to conduct church outreach and generate customized AI Sermon Assistant (RAG) proposals.

---

## ⚡ How to Load in Google Chrome (Developer Mode)

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. In the top-right corner, toggle **Developer mode** to **ON**.
4. Click **Load unpacked** in the top-left corner.
5. Select the folder:
   `C:\Users\ACER\Desktop\me\church-ai-email-extension`
6. Click the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the ChurchAI Proposal Hub!

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Page Auto-Scanner:**
   - Click **Scan Current Page** on any church website, YouTube channel, or contact directory to automatically extract the church name and verified email address.

2. **Email Syntax & Typo Validator:**
   - Catches common domain typos (e.g. `@gamil.com` $\to$ `@gmail.com`) and invalid characters that cause "the email addresses are wrong" errors.

3. **Proven Proposal Templates:**
   - **Senior Pastor Angle:** Focuses on sermon preservation, spiritual legacy, and 24/7 care for members facing questions at 2:00 AM.
   - **Media & Tech Team Angle:** Focuses on YouTube library discovery, exact video timestamps, and simple 1-script-tag website embedding.
   - **Quick 3-Sentence Hook:** High-conversion brief pitch for busy pastors.
   - **WhatsApp / DM:** Direct message script for ministries with active social inboxes.

4. **Multi-Currency Pricing:**
   - Supports **USD ($)** for US/International ministries, **NGN (₦)** for Nigerian/African ministries, and **GBP (£)** for UK churches.

5. **1-Click Multi-Channel Dispatch:**
   - **Copy Proposal:** Formatted subject and body ready to paste anywhere.
   - **Open in Gmail Web:** Automatically launches `mail.google.com` compose window with recipient, subject, and body pre-filled without relying on local Windows mailto handlers.
   - **Desktop Mail:** Launches standard OS mail client.
   - **WhatsApp Direct:** Opens WhatsApp Web / Mobile with the pre-formatted message.
